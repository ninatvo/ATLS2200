Hello!

My name is Nina Vo and I am a senior at CU Boulder majoring in Computer Science and minoring in Creative Technology and Design.

I have professional experience in frontend development using Angular and React, creating intuitive GUIs for satellite ground stations in AstroUX. My passion lies in bridging the gap between design and development to create intuitive and accessible user experiences.
