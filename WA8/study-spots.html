Recommended Study Locations

Norlin Library

Location: 1720 Pleasant St, Boulder

Quiet Level: Varies by floor

Hours: 7:30AM - 10PM M-T, 7:30AM - 6PM F, 10AM - 6PM Sat, 10AM - 10PM Sun

Description: CU's main library with multiple floors, quiet study areas, and group collaboration spaces. Great for research and focused studying.

Best For: Long study sessions, group projects, research

CSEL

Location: Engineering Center

Quiet Level: Noisy during peak hours

Hours: 24/7 with CU Card

Description: Dedicated CS study space with large tables, white boards, and study rooms.

Best For: CS students, coding, group projects

UMC Rooftop

Location: University Memorial Center

Quiet Level: Quiet

Hours: Daylight hours

Description: Outdoor study space with beautiful views. Great for a study break or outdoor group sessions.

Best For: Outdoor study, group discussions, breaks

Benson Earth Science Library

Location: Benson Earth Sciences Building

Quiet Level: Quiet

Hours: 9AM - 8PM M-T, 9AM - 5PM F, Closed Sat, 12PM - 8PM Sun

Description: Quiet library in the Earth Sciences building

Best For: Silent studying

CASE

Location: Center for Academic Success and Engagement (CASE) Building

Quiet Level: Moderate during peak hours

Hours: 7:30 AM - 5:00 PM M-F

Description: Building with lots of tables and conference rooms to study.

Best For: Casual study, group meetings

Alpine Modern Cafe

Location: 904 College Ave, Boulder

Quiet Level: Moderate to Noisy

Hours: 7AM - 4PM

Description: Cute cafe slightly off campus to study at, accessible by HOP bus

Best For: Casual study, group meetings, breaks
